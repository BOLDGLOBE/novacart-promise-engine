Accounts & delivery location

Tell us where you are.

NOVA CART is local commerce. Your delivery location decides which shops you can order from, which ones we will re-route to when something runs out, and which city you are shown at all. It is the first thing we ask for, and the last thing we need from you on every order.

Why location is the whole product
  • A store is only useful to you if it is inside your delivery radius — we compute real distance, not a city filter.
  • When the Promise Engine has to re-route a failed line, it searches your area, not the whole city.
  • Demand forecasts are per micro-market, so your location changes which stock data matters.
  • State is derived from your PIN code, so it can never contradict your address.
This is a front-end prototype. Accounts are stored in your browser only (localStorage) and passwords are salted and SHA-256 hashed, but there is no server — so this is not real authentication. Never use a real password here.
